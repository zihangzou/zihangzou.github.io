Zihang Zou, Ph.D.

I received my Ph.D. from the University of Central Florida, advised by Liqiang Wang. My research asks how neural networks can learn and act reliably under legal, domain, and physical constraints.

I am currently working on physical scenarios and simulation, with broader interests in efficient and lifelong learning.

Physical Scenarios & Simulation

I work on physical scenarios and simulation for learning and evaluating intelligent-system behavior. Our vehicle-dynamics work combines tire–pothole impacts, vertical vehicle dynamics, and interactions between vehicles in a closed-loop simulation environment for reinforcement learning.

Efficient Learning

I am interested in helping models learn with less data and computation. This direction asks how to make effective use of available information while reducing the cost of training and adaptation.

Lifelong Learning

I am interested in models that continue learning as tasks and environments change. The central challenge is to acquire new capabilities while retaining useful knowledge from earlier experience.
