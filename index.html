Grounded Reasoning & Lawful Autonomy

AI Copyright & Data Ownership

How can ownership be verified when personal data is used to train neural networks, and what happens when generative models reproduce copyrighted work? These papers examine two stages of this problem: unauthorized use during training and plagiarism or ownership ambiguity during generation.

2025ICCVFirst author

Generation-time plagiarism & ownership ambiguity

Attention to Neural Plagiarism: Diffusion Models Can Plagiarize Your Copyrighted Images!

Zihang Zou, Boqing Gong, Liqiang Wang

From the abstract

In this paper, we highlight a critical threat posed by emerging neural models--data plagiarism. We demonstrate how modern neural models (e.g., diffusion models) can effortlessly replicate copyrighted images, even when protected by advanced watermarking techniques.
