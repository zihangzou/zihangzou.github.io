I received my Ph.D. from the University of Central Florida, advised by Liqiang Wang.

I study how to protect data ownership and identify plagiarism in neural networks, and how to make learned behavior respect legal and physical constraints. I am currently working on physical scenarios and simulation, with an interest in learning efficiently and adapting over time.

Copyright Protection & Data Plagiarism

I study copyright protection and data plagiarism as fundamental challenges in neural networks and generative AI. My research asks how data ownership can be protected and verified when models learn from, transform, and reproduce data, connecting the use of training data with the ownership of generated content.

Law & Physics Compliance

I study how neural networks can comply with laws, domain rules, and physical constraints. My research connects these requirements with learning, reasoning, and decision-making, using physical scenarios and simulation to study whether model behavior remains compliant in context.
